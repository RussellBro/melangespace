Scalable Characterization of Unexplored Terrain

The world is returning to the Moon, yet we have no data about it.

Our penetrator probes emplace a hundred instruments below the surface in a single mission and return water, temperature, and seismic data from where rovers can't reach.

Data is the foundation to a Moon Base and a growing lunar economy. We exist to discover that data accessibly for humanity to continue its expansion beyond Earth.
